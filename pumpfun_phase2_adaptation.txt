STEPS (in order)
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

STEP 1 — NARRATIVE SCORER (filter BOT FACTORIES)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────
Before any gate analysis, run the narrative scorer:

• Collect all mentions of the candidate coin (X/Reddit, pump.fun,
  DEX screener)
• Count:
  - total mentions (M)
  - unique authors (A)
  - bot-farm indicator: if M/A > 3, flag as coordinated bot signal
• Filter rules:
  - If bot-farm detected (M/A > 5): IGNORE candidate, do NOT evaluate
    further (bot signals override everything)
  - If M/A > 3 but <= 5: warn "coordinated narrative detected",
    proceed with caution
• Log: [NARRATIVE] <coin> mentions=<M>, unique_authors=<A>, ratio=<M/A>

Why: bot farms create artificial volume, fake "crypto operator" signals,
and coordinated buys before the rug. Reject them at the narrative level.

STEP 2 — PUMP PROGRESSION FILTER (CHARGE PRE-SURGE)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────
Filter for the charge-before-the-surge window:

• Collect progress_pct (5m), price, buys_5m, sells_5m
• If candidate has NOT yet entered its 5-minute charge window:
  - Require: buys_5m >= 3, progress_pct >= 5% (just entering),
    is_pump = true
  - If candidate is still in the 0-5% window: SKIP (too early)
  - If candidate is already > 30% in 5m: SKIP (already left the room)
• This catches pumps that are ready to charge but not yet in the
  mainstream — the entry window before others notice.

STEP 3 — HARD GATES (9-layer deep dive, in order)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────
Apply gates in order. First gate that fails SKIPS.

  Gate 1 — Holders: >=45
    Source: RugCheck (api.rugcheck.xyz)
  Gate 2 — Top-10 share: <=25% (leaves out known exchange/pool accounts)
  Gate 3 — Unique wallets: >=15 (not the same as holders)
  Gate 4 — Buys: >=3 bu8es in last 5 minutes
  Gate 5 — Bonding-curve progress: 8% to 70%
    Coin that left the pump curve counts as 100%
    Coin still on curve: progress = SOL in curve / ~85 SOL
  Gate 6 — Developer: holds <=15%
    Sources: RugCheck dev wallet share, GasStation
  Gate 7 — rug_risk: <=0.8 (GrokHOOD Safety Score)
  Gate 8 — Mint & freeze: both revoked
  Gate 9 — Security: no risky token extensions

Grok signals for each gate:
  sources: "RugCheck",
"GrokHOOD",
"DEX Screener",
"pump.fun /v1/stats",
"X/Reddit activity"

Step log: [SKIP <gate>] <field> <value> vs threshold
  Example: [SKIP 2] top-10 share 31% > 25%
  Example: [SKIP 5] curve 78% > 70%
  Example: [SKIP 3] wallets 4 < 15

STEP 4 — VOTING (12-bot desk)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────
• 12 bots vote BUY or STAND DOWN per candidate
• BUY bar: 6 of 12 votes (voters top out at 6 or 7 of 12)
• Consensus: 5+ bots agree, highest conviction wins
• If bot-farm detected at Step 1: override all votes with STAND DOWN
• Analyzer score: weighted analyst_score * (1 + 0.2 * is_pump)

Step log:
  [VOTE] <coin>: 6/12 BUY (analyst 74, GrokHOOD 0.42)
  [SKIP VOTING] <coin> bot-farm detected, narrative ratio 8.5x

STEP 5 — SIZING & EXECUTION (executor role)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────
Base currency: SOL (simulated paper, not USDC)
Buy size: 0.05 SOL (one position, paper mode)
One open position max

Slippage: 15% buy, 5% sell (measured, not simulated)
Entry price = SOL actually spent / tokens received
Exit price = SOL actually received / tokens sold

Fees (applied to P&L, counted as cash costs):
  • 1% pump.fun protocol fee
  • 0.001015 SOL flat per swap leg
  These are NEVER taken off P&L as a separate discount —
  they AFFECT the entry price and exit price.

EXIT LADDER — checked every hold
──────────────────────────────────
Tier 1: sell 50% at 2x entry, once
Tier 2: sell all remaining at 3x
Fallback: sell all at -8% (0.92x trigger) — primary stop
Trailing stop: 2.5x activation, 15% drawdown from high
  (e.g., peak 3.00x → trail high 7.50x → trigger 6.38x)

STEP 6 — STAND DOWN & LOG
──────────────────────────────────────────────────
• [PASS <coin>] — <n>/9 gates, votes <v>/12, analyst <a>, GrokHOOD r,
  narrative ratio <M/A>
• [SKIP <gate>] <field> <value> vs threshold — <source>
• [STAND DOWN] — no new candidates this run; paper pool intact
• Log each decision with: coin, timestamp, decision, reason, gates,
  votes, narrative score
